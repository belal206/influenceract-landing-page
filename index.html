?
What makes InfluencerAct really different?+

We kind of bundle creator strategy, content, campaign coordination, and performance measurement into one ongoing process, not just separate tasks. We begin with your audience, and your business objective, then we set a clear role for each creator and each platform within the campaign.

How do you pick the right creators?+

We don’t just chase follower counts, because that’s not the whole picture. We look at audience fit engagement quality, content tone, category relevance and overall credibility. Then we make a shortlist based on your brief, and we align with you before anything moves into execution.

What budget do I need to start?+

Each campaign comes with its own budget needs. The costs depend on who we choose, the platforms, deliverables, usage rights, production requirements and timing too. You share your expected range and we can talk through a scope that matches what you’re trying to achieve.

Which platforms do you work across?+

We run campaigns across Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, Moj, etc. The best combination depends on your audience, the message, and the objective, not on the idea of being present everywhere, all the time.

How long does a campaign take?+

It really depends on how many creators are involved, what content is required, the approvals flow, and the launch date. We walk you through the key phases up front, before the campaign starts, so responsibilities and milestones don’t get fuzzy.

How will you measure a campaign’s success?+

We define success metrics before launch, like upfront, not after. Depending on your objective, it might include reach, engagement, views, tracked clicks, leads, or conversions. Reporting then lays out the results clearly, plus the insights that help steer your next campaign.
