# InfluencerAct redesign references

The redesign uses original layouts and the InfluencerAct palette. Reference sites were researched on 16 October 2026; their branding, client work, copy and proprietary assets are not reproduced.

| Section | Reference and principle | InfluencerAct treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening campaign form | User-supplied Qoruz banner reference — rounded split panel, diagonal purple bands, compact paired fields | InfluencerAct story, existing testimonial and recognition on dark purple; a white, single-page call-enquiry form with coral controls and a direct email-draft action |
| Brand partners | User-supplied banner reference — restrained social proof below the form | A muted grid of existing InfluencerAct partner logos inside the white form panel |
| Creator culture | [Billion Dollar Boy](https://www.billiondollarboy.com/) — creator-first motion and imagery | A full-width, slightly rotated reel strip with visitor-controlled, muted video |
| Expertise | [Koto](https://koto.com/) — a clear capability index and typographic hierarchy | Four large service rows, with hover imagery and complete details on tap |
| Creator roster | [Billion Dollar Boy](https://www.billiondollarboy.com/) — a human, social-first visual language | Large portraits, overlaid names, swiping, dragging and accessible profile dialogs |
| Process | [Linear](https://linear.app/) — connected stages and concise interface panels | One dark canvas with four numbered stages, native illustrations and expandable details |
| Budget | [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) — a focused input with immediate feedback | A large budget readout, adjustable slider, scope guidance and an action that updates the brief |
| Recognition | [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/) — recognition as a carefully edited index | Six interactive award rows with visual previews and the existing award archive |
| Testimonials | [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) — focused human stories | One large quotation at a time, with manual previous/next controls |
| FAQs and close | [Koto](https://koto.com/) — confident type and a direct next step | A calm question index followed by the oversized lavender “Your Move” footer |

Palette: warm cream `#f8f5ef`, deep plum `#1d0834`, logo purple `#4f00b3`, lavender `#dac8f3`, coral `#f36e52`.

Typography: locally bundled Space Grotesk from Google Fonts throughout the page. Headings and subheadings share consistent semibold styling, body text uses regular weight, and labels and controls use medium weight. Serif and italic font changes have been removed. The font license is included beside the bundled files.

Photography, campaign imagery, creator portraits, videos, testimonials and awards are retained from the existing local InfluencerAct assets. Video poster frames are extracted from the actual bundled films. Motion is muted, requires visitor play or a mouse hover, and respects reduced-motion preferences for automatic hover previews.

The existing logo artwork is unchanged. The website theme uses logo purple with deep-purple backgrounds, coral accents, and continuous cream/lavender surfaces. Green and yellow accent colors were removed. Coral recurs in banner checks and form controls, underlines, service markers, reel controls, award callout, quotation mark and footer arrow.
